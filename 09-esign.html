How Aadhaar eSign works

1. Upload document
Select the PDF you want to sign.

2. Verify with OTP
An OTP is sent to your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

3. Sign & download
Your digital signature is embedded in the PDF with a timestamp and certificate.

Legal validity

Aadhaar eSign is legally valid under the IT Act 2000 (Section 3A) and is equivalent to a handwritten signature for most purposes.
